EXTRALIMO hospitality staffing

Hospitality struggles to find the right extra staff; candidates lack visibility. EXTRALIMO connects them — but it's not a self-service job board: the value is the human touch, a small team of admins who select, propose and support.

Live at extralimo.portfolio-sbrt.com (52 commits, updated June 2026). Built with his co-founders.

Visit the site

The solution

A web platform built around 3 roles (candidate / business / admin) and one central object, the “match” (suggested → in progress → contact unlocked → placed). Privacy by design: contact details stay hidden until an admin unlocks the match. Public mobile-first sign-ups, GDPR consent.

Key outcomes

  • Three roles (candidate / business / admin) around a “match” object
  • Privacy by design: contact details hidden until an admin unlocks them
  • Matches created manually, placement based on contract type and role fit
  • Public mobile-first sign-ups, GDPR consent
  • Business model: commission per contract (a percentage of the placement)
