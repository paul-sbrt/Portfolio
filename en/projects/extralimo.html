The idea

We find them, you shine.

01 — Why

Hospitality struggles to find the right extras; candidates lack visibility. EXTRALIMO connects them — but it's not a self-service job board: the value is the human intermediation that guarantees a relevant match.

“ Not one more job board: a match validated by a human. ”

Gallery —

02 — The approach

a.Three roles, one “match”

Candidate, company and admin revolve around a single connection.

b.Confidentiality by design

Contact details stay hidden until an admin unlocks them.

c.Human intermediation

Matches created manually, by contract type and role affinity.

d.Mobile-first, GDPR

Public sign-ups designed mobile-first, with GDPR consent.

03 — The result

Validated connectionA match created and validated by an admin, not self-service.
Protected contact detailsUnlocked only once the match is confirmed.
Placement by affinityBy contract type and target role.
Commission modelPaid per placed contract (percentage).
