A second brain, augmented by AI

DUMP iOS second brain

You capture an idea, a note or a voice memo on the fly — and then you lose it, or you can't find it in any usable shape. DUMP is a cognitive assistant: you speak or type, messy and unfiltered, and the AI sorts it automatically. Especially handy for minds that overflow with ideas and scatter easily (ADHD included), as an everyday organization tool. Paul built it for himself first.

On TestFlight (build 2) — Paul's most active project (503 commits, updated June 2026), design system v2, production-ready widgets.

The solution

A native iOS app that turns a raw stream into sorted notes: text and voice capture (Whisper transcription), AI classification (Claude) into ACTION / INFO / IDEA / MISC categories and urgency levels, connection suggestions between notes, anonymized entity extraction, dated reminders, and learning from your corrections. Zero-friction onboarding (no account), widgets and a share extension.

Points clés

  • Text + voice capture, with Whisper transcription of voice memos
  • Automatic classification by Claude (ACTION / INFO / IDEA / MISC + custom categories it learns) and urgency levels
  • Connection suggestions between notes and entity extraction (anonymized before sending)
  • Dated reminders, an impulsivity buffer, and learning from the user's corrections
  • Freemium model: free up to 25 AI classifications/week, unlimited with premium via StoreKit 2
