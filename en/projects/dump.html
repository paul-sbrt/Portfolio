DUMP

A second brain, sorted by AI

DUMP is a second brain: you throw your thoughts in on the fly, by voice, by text, a link, a photo, and it sorts them, connects them and reminds you at the right moment. Not one more notebook, a brain that tidies up for you.

DUMP
D

The idea

Empty your head, and let the app handle the rest.

01 Why

When you have too many ideas, you lose half of them. They show up anytime, you tell yourself « I'll write it down later », and they evaporate. DUMP came from that, mine first. The idea: a place to dump everything without thinking, and let the app do the sorting. Made for minds that run too fast, ADHD and gifted profiles.

“ Never let an idea sleep again. ”

02 The approach

a.Capture without friction

Voice, text, link, note, photo. What matters is getting the idea out of your head in a second. On-device OCR reads images, dictation transcribes on the fly.

b.Sort with AI

Claude classifies every note (action, info, idea) and gives it an urgency level. You no longer file things, you just drop them.

c.Connect the ideas

The app weaves clusters between notes and resurfaces what was dormant, at the right moment (reminders, morning recaps).

d.Privacy, not optional

Before anything reaches the AI, sensitive data (names, emails, numbers) is detected and anonymized on the device. The keys stay server-side, never in the app.

Where it stands

DUMP is built natively in Swift/SwiftUI (SwiftData, Cloudflare Workers for the AI proxy). Currently in testing on TestFlight, it's a living project I use and improve continuously.
