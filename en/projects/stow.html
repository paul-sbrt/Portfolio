The idea

The vault for your group trips

01 Why

On group trips, documents are scattered across WhatsApp and impossible to find at the wrong moment, with no offline access at customs or on arrival. STOW turns departure stress into calm: a shared vault, up to date, reachable even offline.

“ No more PDF lost between a meme and a 4-minute voice note. ”

Gallery

02 The approach

a.Offline first

Key documents stay reachable without a network customs, airport, arrival.

b.Shared by design

Each group member contributes their own documents; everyone can access them.

c.Zero setup

Quick Apple or Google login, free useful from the first second.

d.Every doc in its place

No more mixing with the group chat: clear, dedicated storage.

03 The result

Shared tripEach member adds their documents to the shared vault.
Offline accessKey documents available without a connection.
Clear storageEvery document in its place, no more losing it in the chat.
Free, frictionlessApple or Google login, ready in seconds.
