The vault for your group trips

STOW travel worry-free

On a group trip, documents (tickets, bookings, passport photos) get scattered across WhatsApp and are impossible to find at the worst moment, with no offline access at customs or on arrival. STOW is a shared vault for travel documents.

Published on iOS AND Android — “Stow — Voyagez serein” (v1.2), available on the App Store and Google Play Store.

App StoreVisit the site

The solution

Create a trip, invite your friends, and everyone adds their documents (PDFs, screenshots, passport photos). The important ones stay accessible even with no signal. Quick Apple or Google sign-in, files ready in two taps.

Key outcomes

  • Shared trip: every member of the group contributes their own documents
  • Offline access to important documents (customs, an airport with no signal)
  • Every document in its place — no more mixing with the group chat
  • Quick Apple or Google sign-in, free

Want to try it?

The app is available for free on the App Store and the Google Play Store.
