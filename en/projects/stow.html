STOW

The vault for group trips

STOW is the vault for group trips: every important document, tickets, bookings, passports, in one place, shared with the group, and available even offline.

The idea

WhatsApp is for memes. STOW is for your tickets.

01 Why

The idea came from a real trip with friends. Digging for a PDF lost in WhatsApp exactly when you need it, at customs, in the airport basement, with no signal, is the kind of mess that only hits at the worst moment. I wanted a dedicated place where only the trip's documents live, reachable by the whole group.

“ Your travel documents, all in one place. Even offline. ”

02 The approach

a.Tidy, together

Create a trip, invite your companions with a code, and everyone adds their documents. The whole group reaches the same files.

b.Offline for real

No Wi-Fi at customs? The documents stay accessible, downloaded locally, readable with no signal.

c.Every doc in its place

Configurable document types, a native PDF viewer, planning and activities, everything to organize the whole trip.

d.Radically simple

Apple or Google sign-in, biometric lock, files added in two taps.

Where it stands

STOW is built in Flutter (iOS and Android from a single codebase), on Firebase. Published on the App Store, with its own site. A product taken from concept all the way to release.
